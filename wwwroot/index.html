Generator Source Hardware Warning

You are switching the DPLL Generator Source to DDS (AD9833).

CRITICAL HARDWARE REQUIREMENT: Ensure the physical resistor jumper on the PCB is routed to the AD9833 DDS output, NOT to the CD4046 VCO!

Operating without routing the hardware connections properly will leave the transducer without driving signals and break feedback tracking.
